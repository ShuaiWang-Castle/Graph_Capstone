# Fixed metric-SDP study: audited descriptive release

This is a capstone research draft, not an accepted paper or a claim of top-conference novelty. The formal comparison fixed 43 whole-graph cases and five arms before measurement: U (original), S (optimized two-copy pairs plus uniform collective copies), D (degree/profile copies), SD (S to a fixed point, then D), and SW (S, then full-pair degree-anchor PSD). All arms used the same discovery bank, direct metric-SDP backend, original-normalized verified width target of at most 1/1000, 300-second compute budget and monitored 6 GiB RSS limit. Complete compute includes discovery, preprocessing, quotient preparation, numerical solving and exact bound repair/verification. The [configuration](experiments/extensions/metric_sdp_formal/config.json) and [formal freeze](experiments/extensions/metric_sdp_formal/formal-freeze.json) identify the fixed study.

All 215 planned case-arm outcomes were retained: 182 independently replayed, exactly verified targets and 33 full-wall-limit unresolved outcomes. There were no unexecuted tasks. Verified/43 counts are U 37, S 37, D 36, SD 36, SW 36. The formal [summary](analysis/metric_sdp/formal/audited-20260929-v1/summary.json), [215 job rows](analysis/metric_sdp/formal/audited-20260929-v1/job-rows.json), [86 primary pair rows](analysis/metric_sdp/formal/audited-20260929-v1/primary-pair-rows.json), [collection manifest](analysis/metric_sdp/formal/audited-20260929-v1/collection-manifest.json), and [independent exact-replay closeout](analysis/metric_sdp/formal_audit/audit-20260929-v1/closeout.json) are preserved here. The audit was performed by a separate research agent; it is not external peer review.

Time ratios use only pairs meeting the *same exactly verified* target; no timeout budget is imputed as a completion time. All 43 cases remain in each outcome denominator. For SD/SW, seven qualified ratios exceed 2 and the worst is 98.857642; the jointly qualified range is 0.787373–98.857642. The D stage makes no additional vertex removal after S in 31/43 cases. SW has no larger final quotient in any case and a smaller one in seven. These correlated controlled/development cases do **not** establish a robust complete-cost advantage for SD. They do not support an IID confidence interval or a general solver-speed claim. See the [full distribution](figures/metric-sdp-formal-v2/paired-target-cost.png) and the [working manuscript](manuscript/main.pdf).

The same-block D certificate is mathematically covered by full-pair W; the implementation's current W runtime is not an intrinsic lower bound for all W algorithms. Existing signed-Laplacian, copy/improving-map and collective preprocessing ideas also constrain the novelty claim. A proposed general signed-row-mass anchor remains prospective; its isolated mathematical examples do not show real-data utility or superiority over adaptive prior methods. This study leaves both complete-cost utility and joint-contribution novelty open.

This release contains the exact program source and descriptive output files, but omits the 1.3 GB formal raw-run tree, original third-party SNAP archives, archived third-party papers/skills and vendored native KaPoCE.
